# font assets

## font.small

Small pixel font, caps 5 px. Lowercase renders as caps.

- File: `public/assets/ui/font_small.png`
- Cells: 5 x 6, glyphs in order: `0123456789ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ.,!?:-+/%'()<>#* `
- White glyphs; the game tints and outlines them.
- Outline: 1 px #1e1526 where the art has an outline
- Colors (1): #ffffff

## font.big

Bold pixel font, caps 7 px, for titles and numbers.

- File: `public/assets/ui/font_big.png`
- Cells: 7 x 8, glyphs in order: `0123456789ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ!?.,:-+/%'x `
- White glyphs; the game tints and outlines them.
- Outline: 1 px #1e1526 where the art has an outline
- Colors (1): #ffffff
